Our commits are explicitly AI-coauthored: Kasper should verify the findings, write the communication himself, and author any implementation/tests submitted as contributions. Reviewing or relabelling a generated patch does not satisfy that rule. This is an actual acceptance constraint, not a hypothetical concern: [#1193](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/pull/1193) was closed after the maintainer flagged its LLM-generated contribution; human-authored [#1211](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/pull/1211), with AI review disclosed, was approved and merged.

## Reproductions and precise traces on main

Repro patch: [`scratch/smoltcp-upstream-29-repros.patch`](../scratch/smoltcp-upstream-29-repros.patch). Tests use upstream socket helpers and actual `process`/`dispatch`, symbolic addresses and virtual time; explicitly select Reno when enabled. No proposed production fix was applied.

```sh
cd /Volumes/git/ts-exit/scratch/smoltcp-upstream-29
cargo +1.95.0 test -j 4 --lib --features socket-tcp-reno,socket-tcp-cubic \
  brief29 -- --test-threads=1
```

Result: **2 pass, 3 fail**. Two failures are bugs; the third asserts desired NewReno behavior. [Full output](../scratch/smoltcp-upstream-29-tests.txt). With `--skip brief29`, all **702 existing library tests pass**.

**B1 — failed device emission discards fast retransmission (still a bug).** Send four six-byte segments, then three duplicate ACKs. `dispatch` enters fast recovery and prepares the oldest segment; the emit callback returns `Err(())`. A successful retry emits **zero** packets and the timer is `Idle { keep_alive_at: None }`. With no unsent data, keepalive or further peer traffic, recovery has no deadline. Test: `brief29_bug_fast_retransmit_survives_failed_emit`.

Unmodified-main trace: [`tcp.rs:2566–2585`](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/blob/87b463f50c15f30e4cd2e2725a3bcd5cb883e4d9/src/socket/tcp.rs#L2566) sets `pending_fast_retransmit = true`, then makes the timer idle; **line 2712 clears the flag before line 2864's `emit(...)?`**. An error bypasses successful-emission bookkeeping and RTO arming. This directly contradicts dispatch's stated retry semantics. Our old partial-ACK commit had a failed-emission regression; port that behavior without adding NewReno. In particular, keep a pending repair until successful emission and maintain a retry/deadline without repeatedly reducing cwnd.

**B2 — zero-window reopen loses the RTO (still a bug, additional review 26 finding).** Send 12 bytes; ACK the first six while advertising window zero. Main replaces the running RTO with its probe timer. Reopen the window without advancing ACK: `process` makes the timer idle even though six bytes remain in flight. No retransmission occurs over a simulated hour. Test: `brief29_bug_reopened_window_retains_retransmit_timer`.

Trace: [`tcp.rs:2265–2277`](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/blob/87b463f50c15f30e4cd2e2725a3bcd5cb883e4d9/src/socket/tcp.rs#L2265). The probe-start condition permits replacement after a partial ACK; the reopen branch unconditionally calls `set_for_idle`. Restore a data-retransmission deadline when outstanding data remains. [RFC 6298 §5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6298.html#section-5) supplies the timer reference. This is **not fixed by any of our 13 commits**, so propose it as an independent follow-up. Existing zero-window support does not establish that this transition is correct.

**B3 — Cubic cannot build without std (additional upstream bug).** Minimal compile reproduction:

```sh
cargo +1.95.0 check -j 4 --no-default-features \
  --features medium-ip,proto-ipv4,socket-tcp,socket-tcp-cubic
```

Fails with E0599: `f64::powi` at [`cubic.rs:139`](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/blob/87b463f50c15f30e4cd2e2725a3bcd5cb883e4d9/src/socket/tcp/congestion/cubic.rs#L139) and 166; `mul_add` at line 288. These methods are unavailable in core on the tested compiler. Introduced by #1157 (`c414f07` and `c7595bc`), not by our work; our older implementation used explicit multiplication. [Compiler output](../scratch/smoltcp-upstream-29-no-std-cubic.txt). Reno with no std, no alloc, IPv4/IPv6 and defmt **passes**. A small portability PR needs core-compatible arithmetic and the existing cube-root accuracy test, including checking rounding differences from replacing fused multiply-add. No reason to replace upstream's Cubic algorithm.

**E1 — NewReno feature gap, not surviving go-back-N.** The passing `brief29_control_fast_retransmit_preserves_frontier` confirms one oldest-segment repair, an unchanged 24-byte sent frontier, a running RTO, and no redundant resend. The failing `brief29_feature_newreno_partial_ack_repairs_next_hole` ACKs only the repaired first segment; main sends no next-hole repair. Its ACK path resets dup-ACK count, Reno/Cubic exit recovery on the first new-data ACK, and the socket restarts the ordinary RTO. There is no original-flight `recover` boundary. This is classic Reno behavior; propose [RFC 6582](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6582.html) as an improvement, not a correction to an existing NewReno implementation.

**E2 — recovery RTT inflation, policy distinction.** The passing `brief29_observation_fresh_data_can_sample_recovery_delay` verifies that fast retransmit clears the sample, but fresh data sent at 11 ms starts another. A cumulative ACK at 600 ms records **589 ms SRTT**. Main's `on_send` permits this because the fresh sequence exceeds the high-water mark. Our suppression avoids sampling data held behind recovery holes. However, the timed fresh segment was not retransmitted: [RFC 6298 §3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6298.html#section-3)'s prohibition on sampling retransmitted segments does not by itself prove a violation here. Present it as estimator improvement with evidence, not a confirmed protocol bug.

## Upstream survey and fit

- **SACK:** [#256](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/issues/256) remains open; receiver hints originally landed via #266/`ed8dce0` in 2018. [#1146](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/issues/1146) is another open request. Open **#1195** improves emitted SACK ranges, not the sending socket's scoreboard. Its author JPDye says sender recovery is implemented privately and awaiting cleanup. Coordinate with that work first. Missing sender SACK is a recognized feature gap, not a reason to claim every lost burst is a protocol bug.
- **NewReno/fast recovery:** [#558](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/pull/558) closed without merging; Dirbaio expressed interest, so closure is not evidence of rejection. [#1149](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/pull/1149)'s combined overhaul was superseded by the separately merged API, Reno, Cubic and fast-retransmit PRs #1154–1157 in June 2026. In #1155, Dirbaio requested a cwnd-exemption regression and green CI. In #1157 he fixed corner cases and welcomed the work.
- **Pacing:** [#1090](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/pull/1090), a BBR migration, remains open and explicitly identifies absent pacing as a blocker. This establishes demand, not maintainer approval of our pacer or its 1.2 factor. [#949](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/issues/949)'s large-buffer throughput collapse resembles our workload; maintainer advice covered device backpressure, `max_burst_size`, and enabling congestion control. It does not establish the same root cause.
- **RTO:** [#1190](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/issues/1190) is closed by **#1211**, merged October 3. The rejected build-time #1193 was a contribution-policy issue; runtime configurability itself was accepted. Keep the 1 s library default and tune the application using the existing API.
- **Size/review:** no published line-count ceiling. Accepted examples: #1154 +211/−63 across six files; #1156 +353/−22 in one file; #907 +693/−4 across seven files. Much is tests. In [#907](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/pull/907), maintainers requested feature-gated enum state, removal of unrelated changes, and a squashed commit; soft-float size/runtime overhead mattered. [#450](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/pull/450)'s catch-all SACK/CC draft closed, with contributor discussion advocating independently reviewable phases. Small coherent patches are an inference from these precedents, not a formal size rule.
- **Cadence:** git tags show 0.12.0 on 2024-11-28, 0.13.0 on 2026-03-20, 0.13.1 on 2026-04-30, 0.14.0 on 2026-08-17. Releases are irregular; do not assume a near-term crate release for merged fixes. [Release history](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/releases).

[README](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/blob/87b463f50c15f30e4cd2e2725a3bcd5cb883e4d9/README.md) prioritizes simplicity, robustness and bare-metal use; no heap is required. Main declares Rust **1.91**, edition 2024, and CI tests MSRV/stable/nightly. `std` enables `alloc`; both are optional. Separate `socket-tcp-reno` and `socket-tcp-cubic` features select available controllers. Cubic wins the default selection if both are enabled, but GeiserX explicitly selects Reno. Main's CI includes congestion features in std tests, while its no_std feature matrix omits them, explaining B3's coverage gap. It also contains a nightly **16-bit MSP430** build: fixed-memory portability concerns extend beyond 32-bit. [Cargo configuration](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/blob/87b463f50c15f30e4cd2e2725a3bcd5cb883e4d9/Cargo.toml), [CI](https://github.com/smoltcp-rs/smoltcp/blob/87b463f50c15f30e4cd2e2725a3bcd5cb883e4d9/ci.sh).

**The scoreboard fits the allocation philosophy:** `heapless::Vec<_, 32>` stores 256 bytes of range endpoints plus length metadata inline, requires neither std nor alloc, and main already depends on heapless 0.9. Capacity overflow forgets advisory information rather than freeing unacknowledged bytes. Nevertheless, unconditional per-socket cost and bounded nested scans need justification on small devices. Propose an opt-in sender-recovery feature and a configurable range limit following existing `build.rs`/feature conventions, subject to maintainer preference. `socket-tcp-sack`/`socket-tcp-pacing` would be proposed names, not existing features. Receive `assembler-max-segment-count-32` already exists; no PR is needed to choose it locally.

For every implementation PR, require MSRV 1.91, focused regressions and the relevant existing CI matrix; check with neither std nor alloc, with defmt, IPv4/IPv6, neither/each/both controllers, and 32-bit `thumbv7em-none-eabi` compilation. Audit arithmetic against the project's 16-bit configuration too. The **actual checks here** used 1.95 on aarch64 macOS; MSRV and 32-bit targets were not installed/tested, and no port was implemented.

For recovery/pacing, adapt upstream's existing `tests/netsim.rs` fixed-bandwidth/tail-drop framework rather than importing our whole forwarder bench. Benchmark current-main Reno and Cubic independently, then each change: 25 ms, 50/150/250 Mbit/s, shallow queues and 256 KiB/1–2 MiB windows, one/eight flows, plus no-loss, reordering and random-loss controls. Report goodput, longest pause, retransmitted bytes, fairness and latency. Our `BW_MBPS`/`QUEUE_KB` bench and Brief 24 results motivate the work; **they do not measure a main→patched-main improvement**. No new throughput benchmark was run for this report.

**An issue/discussion first is wiser for NewReno/SACK and pacing**, and the adopted policy asks for prior discussion of features. Prefer a short human-written message linking #256/#1195, describing the measured workload, fixed-size design, RFC subset and planned tests, and asking whether to collaborate on the existing sender work. Raise pacing separately; do not open a duplicate RTO issue. Small confirmed bugs can proceed as independent human-authored fixes after Kasper verifies their reproductions.

Drop obsolete Reno/Cubic/cwnd/RTO backports. Keep deployment choices, 200 ms policy, baseline-RTT bypass, 1.2 factor, IW10, bench tooling and wrapper clock fix local unless separately justified. Suppressing recovery samples needs its own agreement and measurement; it must not silently discard useful samples indefinitely. Our scoreboard lacks rescue retransmission/RACK, so label it a bounded subset, not complete RFC 6675 compliance.

Porting the useful remainder is roughly **5–8 days for NewReno + hardened SACK**, **8–13 with pacing**, plus the independent bug fixes and integration/benchmark time. Main's changed controller API, persistent sent frontier, segmentation offload and RTO estimator mean this is a deliberate adaptation/human reimplementation, not a clean cherry-pick. Prefer main's RTO API and current algorithms; a temporary crates.io override must pin a source satisfying GeiserX's 0.14 dependency and explicitly restore the desired socket configuration.

No PRs, comments, pushes, live-node changes or production-code edits were made. Only this report and scratch research/test artifacts were written.
